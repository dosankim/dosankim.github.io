Privacy Policy – My Poster

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Who uses this app

My Poster is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own accounts.

Data we access

With the owner's consent via OAuth, the app accesses only what is needed to upload videos and their metadata (title, description, privacy settings) to the owner's YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X accounts.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on the owner's private server and are never shared.

Use

Data is used only to upload the owner's own videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Google API Services

My Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and in the security/apps settings of Instagram, TikTok and X.

Deletion

Stored tokens and data are deleted on request or when access is revoked.
